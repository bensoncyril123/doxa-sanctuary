Small Groups

Why Small Groups?

Church Is Better Together

Sunday service is where we gather as one body, but small groups are where real life happens. They are where you are known by name, prayed for by people who care, and challenged to grow. At DOXA Sanctuary, our small groups are not programs; they are families within the family.

"Small groups are where real life happens."

Whether you're new to faith or have walked with God for years, there is a group for you. Choose the one that fits your season, your interests, or your calling, and show up ready to grow.

6 Groups Available
Weekly Meetings
In Person Bronx & Online
Free No Cost to Join

Find Your Group

Our Small Groups

Six intentional communities — a place to belong, grow, and make a difference.

01
Faith

Bible Study & Discussion

A relaxed setting to explore Scripture, unpack sermons, and apply God's Word to real life. We go beyond reading — we discuss, question, and discover what the Bible means for us today. Whether you're a lifelong believer or still figuring things out, there's a seat at the table for you.

Weekly · In person & Zoom
02
Prayer

Prayer Circles

An intimate group built entirely around prayer — interceding for one another, our church, and our city. We believe prayer moves mountains and changes hearts, starting with our own. Come with your burdens, your praise, and your faith, and watch God show up.

Weekly · In person & Zoom
03
Life Stage

Life Stage Groups

Young Adults, Married Couples, Men's and Women's Groups — find people in your season who truly get it. Life looks different depending on where you are, and these groups are designed to meet you right there. Real conversations, real support, and friendships that carry you through every chapter.

Bi-weekly · In person · Bronx
04
Growth

Accountability & Growth

Groups of 3 to 5 committed to spiritual discipline, Bible reading, and personal growth — for those who want to go deep. Accountability here isn't about judgment; it's about iron sharpening iron. You'll push each other, celebrate each other, and grow in ways that simply can't happen alone.

Weekly · In person or Zoom
05
Leadership

Leadership Development

For those called to lead. Building servant leaders through teaching, mentorship, and hands-on ministry training. Leadership at DOXA is rooted in character before platform — we develop people who lead with humility, integrity, and a genuine love for others. If you sense a call, this is where it gets shaped.

Bi-weekly · Application-based
06
Outreach

Outreach & Evangelism

Taking the Gospel into the neighborhoods, schools, and streets of the Bronx to pray, serve, and share Jesus. We don't just talk about being the church — we go and be it. From community outreach events to one-on-one conversations, every member of this group is equipped and sent with purpose.

Monthly · Open to all
