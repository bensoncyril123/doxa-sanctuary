Who We Are

DOXA Sanctuary is a family church in the heart of the Bronx, and a branch of TOP Ministries International. We are a home where every person is known, loved, and welcomed. Not a face in a crowd, but a name, a story, and a life that matters. We are a community of believers passionate about faith, family, and the world beyond our doors, committed to raising leaders, building families, and reaching the nations together. Grounded in the Word and moved by love, we believe the Bronx is not just where we are, it is where God has planted us on purpose. Our roots run deep here, and our vision runs further. The Bronx is our starting point. The nations are our destination. Because the Gospel has no borders, and every soul matters. Whatever you are carrying, whatever your story, there is a place for you here. Come home to DOXA.

Community Outreach

We Don't Just Preach It.
We Live It.

At DOXA Sanctuary, we don't wait for the community to come to us; we go to them (Matthew 28:19). We take the love of Christ to the streets, showing up with the Gospel of Jesus Christ, fellowship, and food right where people are (Acts 2:46). Because we believe that faith without action is incomplete (James 2:14–26), and love without presence means nothing (1 Corinthians 14:1).

We are not content to simply hold services on Sunday and close our doors on Monday. We are a church on the move — alive, active, and deeply invested in the wellbeing of the people around us. The Bronx is not just the neighborhood we occupy; it is the community we are called to serve, to love, and to pour into with everything we have.

And beyond the tangible, we bring something that cannot be packaged or handed out: genuine human connection. We sit with people. We listen. We pray. We look them in the eyes and remind them that they are not invisible, that their life has value, and that there is a community right here in the Bronx that has room for them.
