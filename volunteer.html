Why Serve?

Your Life Makes Sense When You Serve

At DOXA Sanctuary, we believe every believer is called to serve. When you step into your gift, you are not just helping run a program — you are building the church, touching lives, and fulfilling your God-given purpose.

Grow in Your Gift

Serving sharpens the gifts God placed in you. Every Sunday is an opportunity to develop and grow into who He called you to be.

Build Community

Your deepest friendships at DOXA will be formed through serving together. There is nothing like being on a team with people chasing the same mission.

Impact the World

What happens at DOXA goes beyond these walls. Every role you fill contributes to a mission that reaches families, the Bronx, and the nations.
