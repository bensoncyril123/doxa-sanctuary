Why Serve?

Your Life Makes Sense When You Serve

At DOXA Sanctuary, we believe every believer is called to serve. When you step into your gift, you are not just helping run a program — you are building the church, touching lives, and fulfilling your God-given purpose.

Grow in Your Gift

Serving sharpens the gifts God placed in you. Every Sunday is an opportunity to develop and grow into who He called you to be.

Build Community

Your deepest friendships at DOXA will be formed through serving together. There is nothing like being on a team with people chasing the same mission.

Impact the World

What happens at DOXA goes beyond these walls. Every role you fill contributes to a mission that reaches families, the Bronx, and the nations.

Get Involved

Explore Our Teams

Hover over a team to learn more — then fill out the sign-up form at the bottom of this page.

Worship Team

Lead the congregation in Spirit-filled praise and worship. Whether you sing or play an instrument, bring your gift to the altar every Sunday.

Welcome Team

Be the first smile someone sees walking through our doors. Greet, usher, and help every guest feel at home from the moment they arrive.

Prayer Team

Intercede for the church and minister to people at the altar after service. Your prayers change lives and shift atmospheres.

Children's Ministry

Teach, mentor, and pour into children aged 3–12 through Bible lessons and activities. Help lay a faith foundation that lasts a lifetime.

Nursery

Care for our youngest members during Sunday service so their parents can worship freely. A heart for babies and toddlers is all you need.

Youth Ministry

Walk alongside teenagers and young adults as a mentor, leader, or helper. Invest in the next generation and help shape their faith and future.

Media & Slides

Run the presentation slides and livestream during Sunday service. Your work behind the screen keeps the congregation connected to every moment.

Sound Team

Operate the soundboard and ensure every voice and instrument is heard clearly. Your work behind the scenes sets the atmosphere for the entire service.

Photography & Media

Capture the moments that tell DOXA's story — services, events, and community life. Share the vision of this house with the world through content and social media.

Events Team

Plan, organise, and execute church events from start to finish. If you love bringing people together and making things happen, this is your team.

Outreach

Take the love of God beyond our walls into the Bronx community. Serve at outreaches, community events, and missions initiatives throughout the year.

Cleaning & Facilities

Keep our sanctuary clean, orderly, and ready for worship. A tidy house honours God and creates a welcoming environment for every person who walks in.
