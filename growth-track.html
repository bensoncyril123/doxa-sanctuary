What Is Growth Track?

A Journey to Purpose

Growth Track is a four-step experience designed to help you connect to DOXA Sanctuary, discover your God-given design, develop your character, and deploy your gifts in meaningful ministry. Each step builds on the last, taking you from first-time guest to fully engaged member of the family.

"For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do." — Ephesians 2:10

Whether you're brand new to DOXA or have been attending for years, Growth Track will help you go deeper, grow stronger, and serve with greater clarity and confidence.

What You'll Walk Away With

Each step unlocks something new for your journey.

Church Membership

Officially become part of the DOXA family and commit to the community.

Your Spiritual Profile

Discover your spiritual gifts, personality type, and God-given passions.

Leadership Foundations

Build the character and skills needed to lead and serve with impact.

Your Place to Serve

Get connected to a ministry team where your gifts will make a real difference.

The Journey

Four Steps to Your Purpose

Each step meets once a month. You can start at any step and complete them in any order.

01

Discover DOXA

Learn who we are, what we believe, and how DOXA Sanctuary came to be. This is where you officially become a member of the family and understand what it means to be part of this community.

1st Sunday monthly
02

Discover Yourself

Uncover the unique way God wired you — your spiritual gifts, personality, passions, and experiences. You'll walk away with a clear picture of how God designed you and why it matters.

2nd Sunday monthly
03

Develop Your Character

Platform without character is dangerous. In Step 3, we dig into what it means to lead and live like Jesus — growing in integrity, humility, and the disciplines that produce lasting fruit.

3rd Sunday monthly
04

Deploy Your Gifts

This is where it all comes together. Connect with a ministry team, find your place to serve, and start making the difference only you can make. You were made for more than a seat.

4th Sunday monthly

Fast Track Option

Complete All 4 Steps in One Day

Can't commit to four separate Sundays? We offer a Fast Track option where you complete all four Growth Track steps in a single afternoon session. Same content, same experience — condensed into one powerful day.
