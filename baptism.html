I Want to Be Baptized

Understanding Baptism

What Is Baptism?

Baptism is an outward expression of an inward decision. It is your public declaration that you have placed your faith in Jesus Christ — that you have died to your old self and risen to a new life in Him.

Just as Jesus was buried and raised from the dead, going under the water and coming back up is a picture of that same story — your old life buried and your new life in Christ celebrated.

"We were therefore buried with him through baptism into death in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead... we too may live a new life." — Romans 6:4

You don't need to be perfect. You just need to have said yes to Jesus. If you have, baptism is your next step.

"Today is the best day to take your next step."

Three Reasons

Why Get Baptized?

Baptism is not about earning God's love — it's about responding to it.

Jesus Did It First

Jesus himself was baptized at the start of his ministry. When we follow him into the water, we are following his example and identifying with his death and resurrection.

It's a Public Declaration

Baptism is your way of telling the world, your family, friends, and church, that you belong to Jesus. It is your faith made visible.

It's a Celebration

At DOXA, we cheer for every person who goes into that water. Baptism is a moment of celebration for you and for the whole church family.

Step by Step

What to Expect

Here is everything you need to know so you can show up ready and confident.

01

Sign Up

Fill out the form below. Our team will reach out to confirm your date and answer any questions.

02

What to Wear

Wear comfortable, dark-colored clothing you don't mind getting wet. You'll change into a provided robe if needed.

03

Go Under the Water

A pastor will pray with you and guide you into the water. You'll go under once and come back up, it takes just a few seconds.

04

Celebrate!

Your church family will celebrate with you. This is your moment, embrace it, share it, and enjoy it.
